## 🌟 Executive Creative Direction & Architecture

**Utkarsh City** is an interactive, cinematic 3D portfolio that unites the atmospheric drama of realistic open-world driving games with the architectural elegance and luxury of Dubai. Built with **React 19**, **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber (R3F)**, **Drei**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **TypeScript**.

- **Atmosphere:** Dubai-inspired golden-hour lighting (`#ffb066`), twilight cyan skyfill, royal-purple accents, coastal waterfront reflections, palm-lined boulevards, and monolithic glass skyscrapers with illuminated window matrices.
- **Personal Character:** Utkarsh Arora depicted in a tailored charcoal-grey formal suit, crisp white shirt, black eyeglasses, and natural posture beside a bespoke luxury sports car.
- **Biometric Hologram Pedestal:** Embedded interactive pedestal showcasing Utkarsh Arora's authentic reference photograph (`public/utkarsh_arora.jpeg`) with live biometric verification telemetry.
- **Luxury Sports Car:** Aerodynamic wide-body supercar featuring glowing LED xenon headlights, real-time forward spotlights, red neon taillight bar, electric-blue underglow, and rotating alloy wheels.
- **Playable Free Drive Mode:** Full WASD / Arrow-key vehicle physics (acceleration, braking, steering, telemetry HUD, digital speedometer, and synthesized engine roar).

## 🎮 Navigation & Keyboard Controls

- **W / Arrow Up:** Accelerate sports car
- **S / Arrow Down:** Reverse / Brake sports car
- **A / Arrow Left:** Steer left
- **D / Arrow Right:** Steer right
- **1 – 9:** Fast-travel instantly to any of the 9 city checkpoints
- **M:** Mute / Unmute procedural ambient audio & engine synth
- **Escape:** Close any open dossier or exit Free Drive mode
- **Toggle 2D View:** Instant switch to responsive accessible 2D layout
